How to label
  1. The day is split into windows of a few hours. Work through them with the arrow keys — the dots show which you have seen. Do not try to scan a whole day at once; the passes are too small to pick out reliably at that width.
  2. In each window, check all three panels. A South Atlantic Anomaly pass shows as a raised region — often much clearer in ddsi or ddsi×hardness than in flux.
  3. Click anywhere in a panel where the pass begins.
  4. Click again where it ends.
  5. Drag either edge to fine-tune, then press Save label.
  6. Repeat for every pass you can see, then Mark day complete.

Arrow keys move the window, shift+arrows change day. Scroll to zoom, shift-drag to pan, Esc clears a selection. All times are UTC. There are typically around 11 passes per day, but some are too weak to see — only label what you can actually identify. Missing an invisible pass is expected and is not an error.
